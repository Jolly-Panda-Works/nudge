# Code of Conduct

We want Nudge to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience, background or language.

**Expected:** be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing other people's private information (including real notification content), and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues that violate this code, and may restrict participation of people who do. Report concerns to the maintainers through a private GitHub message or the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). This text is a short summary in the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
