feat: Nudge v0.9.0 - reminder alerts (M2 section 4)

- Alerts at the reminder time through the Android alarm service, with a
  Snooze 10 min button; alarms are set again after a restart or update,
  and a reminder missed while the phone was off is shown at start-up
- ReminderScheduler keeps alarms in step with open tasks and applies
  snoozes done from an alert while the app was closed
- Kotlin app.nudge.reminders: store, scheduler (exact when allowed,
  otherwise inexact), notifier + channel, alarm/snooze/boot receivers,
  method channel
- Settings > Reminders: alerts and exact-timing state with links to the
  system screens; notification permission asked when a reminder is saved
- wire_capture.py: permissions, receivers, reminder channel in an already
  wired MainActivity
- tests: scheduler, settings and permission flows (fake platform);
  taller widget-test window
- docs: changelog, roadmap, status, privacy, development checklist

Not compiled, tested or run: CI first. Windows has no alerts.
